A 100-word villain monologue for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue" bounty ($2 in USDC). Lord Veylan of the Ashen Spire receives a hero who has come for the Ember Crown — and, between the polished skulls, the ravens' triplicate reports, and the pickled heart of his brother Aldric, quietly gives the crown away. His tiredness is never announced: it lives in the pauses, the repeated Tuesday, and the deflection into chores (the dam, the ledgers). The sting lands in the last line, when he keeps only the little bell — because someone still has to listen for the river.
